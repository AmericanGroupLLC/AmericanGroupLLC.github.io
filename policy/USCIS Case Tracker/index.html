Information We Collect

USCIS Case Tracker does not require users to create an account or sign in to use the application. The app allows users to search publicly available USCIS case status information by entering a USCIS receipt number. If you choose to track a case, the receipt number and related tracking information are stored locally on your device to provide case tracking functionality. American Group LLC does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Local Data Storage

Tracked USCIS receipt numbers, notification preferences, and app settings are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide app functionality and is not associated with a user account.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the application may notify you about updates related to the USCIS cases you choose to track. Notification permissions are optional and can be changed at any time in your device settings.
