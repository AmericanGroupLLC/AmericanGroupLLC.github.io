Information We Collect

USCIS Case Tracker does not require users to create an account and does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or government identification numbers. Users enter a USCIS receipt number to retrieve publicly available case status information directly from official USCIS services.

Stored Information

If you choose to save case numbers within the application, they are stored locally on your device to provide case tracking functionality. American Group LLC does not collect or store your saved case numbers on its own servers.

Notifications

If enabled, the application may send notifications about updates to your saved USCIS cases. Notification permission is optional and can be disabled at any time through your device settings.

Authentication

The application does not require user registration, login, Google Sign-In, or any social media authentication.

Government Disclaimer

USCIS Case Tracker is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), the U.S. Department of Homeland Security, or any other government agency. Official USCIS information is available at https://www.uscis.gov.
