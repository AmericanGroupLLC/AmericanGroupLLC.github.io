Information We Collect

USCIS Case Tracker requires users to sign in using their account credentials. The application collects the minimum information necessary to authenticate users, such as the email address associated with the account. Users may also enter USCIS receipt numbers to retrieve publicly available case status information from official USCIS services. American Group LLC does not sell or share users' personal information.

Stored Information

If you choose to save USCIS receipt numbers within the application, they are stored only to provide case tracking functionality. American Group LLC does not sell users' personal information.

Notifications

If enabled, the application may send notifications regarding updates to tracked USCIS cases. Notification permissions are optional and can be disabled at any time through your device settings.

Authentication

The application requires users to sign in using their account credentials. The app does not support public self-registration, Google Sign-In, or social media authentication. Authentication is used solely to provide secure access to application features.

Government Disclaimer

USCIS Case Tracker is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), the U.S. Department of Homeland Security, or any other government agency. Official USCIS information is available at https://www.uscis.gov.
