01About This App

Case Tracker for USCIS is a personal organizational tool. It helps you keep a private, on-device list of U.S. immigration case receipt numbers, look up the publicly available status of those cases, read publicly published processing-time estimates and agency news, and reach official government portals through direct links.

The app only displays publicly available government information. It does not access any private, restricted, internal, or non-public government system, record, or database. Every piece of government information the app shows originates from a public webpage, public RSS feed, or public status-lookup endpoint that any member of the public can access directly without credentials — the same information you can obtain yourself, free of charge, from the agency websites listed in the next section.

The app does not create, modify, certify, or issue any government record, notice, decision, or determination.

03Information Accuracy & Verification

  • Official sources always take precedence. If information in this app differs from an official government source, the official source is correct.
  • Information may be delayed or stale. Data is retrieved when you open or refresh a screen. Government systems update on their own schedules, and network or agency outages can cause the app to show older data.
  • Processing times are agency estimates, not commitments. They are published historical figures and do not predict your case.
  • The app cannot guarantee completeness or accuracy of any government information, and no outcome of any kind is promised or implied.
  • Verify before acting. Always confirm on uscis.gov — or the relevant agency site — before making any filing, travel, employment, financial, or legal decision.
  • When a live lookup is unavailable, the app labels the result explicitly as not live rather than presenting a substitute as a real status (see §5).

04Automated / AI-Generated Content

Two optional features — "Ask AI" and "Impact Advisor" — generate responses using a third-party large language model (OpenAI) reached through the app's backend service.

  • AI output is not government information and does not come from USCIS or any agency. It is machine-generated text.
  • AI output is not legal advice and may be incomplete, outdated, or factually wrong.
  • Do not submit sensitive personal information — such as Social Security numbers, A-numbers, passport numbers, or financial details — into these features. Text you type is transmitted to the app's backend and on to the model provider to produce a response.
  • Never rely on AI output for a filing, deadline, or legal decision without confirming it against an official government source or a licensed attorney.

05Sample & Demonstration Data

The app ships with a small set of clearly labelled example records used only for offline demonstration and developer testing.

  • No sample or demonstration data is preloaded into your app. A new installation starts completely empty: no tracked cases, no history, no alerts.
  • Any example content that appears is explicitly marked — for instance "Sample data (offline)", "Sample only (not live)", or "Sample data — this is a demonstration case, not a live USCIS status."
  • When a live lookup cannot be completed, the app shows an explicit "Live lookup unavailable … This is NOT a real case status" notice. It never fabricates a plausible-looking status.

08Data Storage & Retention

  • Where. Your case list, alerts, preferences and practice progress are kept in the operating system's standard app-preferences store, inside your app's private sandbox on your own device.
  • Retention. Local data stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes it.
  • No server-side copy of your case list. The app does not upload or back up your tracked cases to the Developer's servers.
  • Fresh-start reset. On upgrade to this release, the app performs a one-time cleanup that removes legacy demonstration cases, legacy sample alerts, and any stale session record, so the app behaves like a clean installation. Your language, notification and onboarding preferences and your Civics Test progress are preserved.
  • Device backups. If you enable your platform's device backup, your OS may include app preference data in that backup under Google's or Apple's policies, not ours.

10Notifications

This app sends no push notifications. It integrates no push messaging service — there is no Firebase Cloud Messaging, no APNs integration, and no third-party push provider in the app.

  • All alerts are generated locally on your device and appear only inside the app's Alerts tab.
  • An alert is created when the app refreshes a case you are tracking and the publicly reported status differs from the value already stored on your device.
  • No notification token, device token, or push identifier is created, stored, or transmitted, because no push service is used.
  • The alert toggles in Settings control only whether these local, in-app entries are generated. Turning them off does not disable any external messaging, because none exists.
  • The app never sends marketing, promotional, or advertising messages.

12Your Rights & Choices

  • Delete individual data. Remove any tracked case from the Case History screen, or clear every tracked case at once from the Profile screen.
  • Delete everything. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.
  • Export your data. Any tracked case's summary can be copied to your clipboard from Case History.
  • Control alerts. Turn local alert generation on or off in Settings.
  • Use the app without an account. No sign-in is required or offered in this release, and no personal profile is created.
  • Decline optional features. The AI features are entirely optional; if you never open them, nothing is sent to the model provider.
  • Statutory rights. Depending on where you live — for example under the GDPR/UK GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA — you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict processing of your personal information, and to be free from discrimination for exercising them. Because your case data is stored on your device and not in our accounts system, most of these rights are exercised directly in the app. For anything else, or to ask about server-side records, contact us using §17. 13Security

  • Encrypted transport. All network requests use HTTPS/TLS.
  • OS sandboxing. Local data is held in the app's private storage area, isolated from other apps by the operating system.
  • Minimal data surface. The strongest protection is that sensitive data is never collected: no SSNs, A-numbers, passport numbers, biometrics, financial data, documents, or precise location.
  • No credentials for government systems. The app never asks for your myUSCIS or any government account login.
  • Session storage. If sign-in is enabled in a future release, session tokens are held by the authentication SDK in the platform secure store (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
  • Honest limits. No method of electronic transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If your device is lost, unlocked, or compromised, data stored in the app may be accessible.

14Children's Privacy

This app is intended for adults managing their own or their family's immigration matters. It is not directed to children, contains no content designed to appeal to children, and includes no ads or in-app purchases. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us using §17 and we will address it.

15International Users

The app is used worldwide, but the government information it displays concerns United States immigration processes and is published by U.S. federal agencies in English. The Developer's backend and its service providers may process requests in the United States or other countries, meaning your request data may be transferred across borders and handled under laws that differ from those where you live. Where the app offers other languages, translations are provided for convenience; the official English-language government source always prevails.
