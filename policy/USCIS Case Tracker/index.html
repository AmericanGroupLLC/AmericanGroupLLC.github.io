Information We Collect

USCIS Case does not require users to create an account or sign in. The application allows users to search publicly available USCIS case status information by entering a valid USCIS receipt number.

The application retrieves publicly available case status information from the official USCIS Case Status Online service and does not modify or alter any government information.

If you choose to track a case, the receipt number, tracking information, notification preferences, and application settings are stored locally on your device to provide the app's functionality.

American Group LLC does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Local Data Storage

Tracked USCIS receipt numbers, notification preferences, and application settings are stored locally on your device. This information is not associated with a user account and is used only to provide case tracking functionality within the application.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the application may notify you about updates related to the USCIS cases you choose to track. Notification permissions are optional and may be changed at any time through your Android device settings.

How We Use Information

Information stored by the application is used solely to:

  • Search USCIS case status.
  • Track cases you choose to save.
  • Provide notifications for tracked cases (when enabled).
  • Remember your application preferences.
  • Improve application stability and user experience.
