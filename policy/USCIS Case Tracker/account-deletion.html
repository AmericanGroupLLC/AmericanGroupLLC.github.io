This page explains how users of USCIS Case Tracker can request deletion of their account and associated data.

Request Account Deletion

If you would like your account to be permanently deleted, please send an email to:

Email: contact@safecodeg.com

Please use the subject:

Account Deletion Request

Include the email address associated with your USCIS Case Tracker account so we can identify your account.

What Will Be Deleted

  • Your application account.
  • Your saved USCIS tracked cases.
  • Associated account information stored by the application.

What May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained where required by law or for fraud prevention, security, or legal compliance. Any retained data will only be kept for the minimum period required.

Removing Local Data

You can remove locally stored tracked cases at any time by opening the app and navigating to:

Profile → Clear All Tracked Cases

Signing out of the application does not delete your account.

Processing Time

Verified account deletion requests are generally processed within 30 days.
