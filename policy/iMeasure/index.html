iMeasure Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how iMeasure ("iMeasure", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the iMeasure mobile application.

Privacy at a glance: iMeasure is designed to provide measurement and utility features without requiring an account. Sign-in is optional and is not required to use the core application.

1. Information We Collect

You can use the core features of iMeasure as a guest without creating an account.

If you voluntarily create or use an account, iMeasure may process information required for authentication and account management, including:

  • Email address
  • Supabase user/account identifier
  • Name or profile information provided by Google or Apple sign-in, where available

Account information is transmitted to and stored by our authentication service, Supabase, when you choose to use account functionality.

2. Location Information

iMeasure may request access to your device's location for compass and true-north functionality.

Location information is processed on your device and is not transmitted to our servers for account management, advertising, analytics, or other external purposes.

You can deny or revoke location permission through your device settings. Some location-dependent features may not function if permission is denied.

3. Camera and Sensors

iMeasure may request access to the device camera for its measurement features, including the live camera-based measurement interface.

iMeasure may also use device sensors such as the accelerometer, magnetometer, gyroscope, and other supported sensors to provide measurement and orientation functionality.

Camera and sensor information used by these features is processed on the device. iMeasure does not upload camera images, photographs, or sensor readings to its servers.

4. Microphone

The Noise Meter feature may request microphone access to measure environmental sound levels.

Audio is processed on the device to calculate sound-level measurements. iMeasure does not record, store, or transmit microphone audio to our servers.

5. Account and Authentication

Sign-in is completely optional. You can use iMeasure as a guest without creating or signing into an account.

iMeasure may provide email/password, Google, Apple, and anonymous authentication options through Supabase. When you choose to use these services, the information necessary to authenticate your account may be transmitted to the applicable authentication provider.

Authentication and related network communication are transmitted using encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS.

6. Local Storage

iMeasure stores certain application preferences and settings locally on your device. These may include preferences such as theme, language, onboarding state, guest status, and other settings required for the application experience.

Measurement information processed locally by the application is not automatically transmitted to our servers unless a feature explicitly requires an online service.

7. Third-Party Services

iMeasure uses third-party services when required to provide optional account and application functionality.

These services may include:

  • Supabase for optional authentication and related backend services
  • Google authentication services when Google Sign-In is voluntarily used
  • Apple authentication services when Sign in with Apple is voluntarily used

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Advertising and Analytics

The current release of iMeasure does not use advertising SDKs, does not display third-party advertisements, and does not use Advertising ID.

The current release also does not use third-party analytics or crash reporting services such as Firebase Crashlytics, Sentry, PostHog, AppsFlyer, or OneSignal.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Account information may be processed by authentication and infrastructure providers when you voluntarily use account functionality.

Camera, microphone, location, and sensor information used for measurement features is processed on the device and is not uploaded to our servers.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by iMeasure. Network communication with supported online services uses encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Account Deletion

iMeasure supports optional account creation. If you create an account, you may request deletion of your account and associated account information.

To request account deletion, contact us at:

contact@safecodeg.com

We will process valid account deletion requests in accordance with applicable requirements and our ability to verify the request.

Note: Account deletion functionality and the public deletion-request process must be available before account creation is offered in a Google Play release where Google's account deletion requirements apply.

12. Your Choices

  • You may use the core application without signing in.
  • You may decline camera, microphone, and location permissions.
  • You may revoke previously granted permissions through Android settings.
  • You may choose whether to create or use an account.
  • You may request account deletion by contacting us.

13. Children's Privacy

iMeasure is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your information, or an account deletion request, please contact us:

Email: contact@safecodeg.com
