iMeasure Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how iMeasure ("iMeasure", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the iMeasure mobile application.

Privacy at a glance: iMeasure is designed to provide measurement and utility features without requiring an account. Sign-in is optional and is not required to use the core application.

1. Information We Collect

iMeasure does not require you to create an account to use its core features. If you voluntarily sign in, we may process the information necessary to authenticate and maintain your account, such as your email address and authentication identifier.

Depending on the feature you use and the permissions you grant, iMeasure may access device sensors and location information to provide measurement functionality.

2. Location Information

Some iMeasure features may use precise device location to provide location-dependent measurement or utility functionality.

Location access is permission-based. You can deny or revoke location permission through your device settings. Denying permission may prevent features that require location from functioning.

iMeasure does not require location access simply to use the application's general measurement tools.

3. Camera and Sensors

iMeasure may request access to the device camera and sensors, such as the accelerometer and magnetometer, when required by measurement features.

Camera and sensor access is used to provide the corresponding functionality. You can control these permissions through your device settings.

4. Account and Authentication

Sign-in is optional. Users can use iMeasure as a guest without creating or signing into an account.

If you choose to sign in, authentication services may process your email address and account identifier to provide account functionality.

Authentication and related network communication are transmitted using encrypted connections.

5. Data Storage

iMeasure stores certain application preferences and settings locally on your device, such as preferences required for the application experience.

Information generated while using measurement features may remain on your device unless a feature explicitly requires transmission to an online service.

6. Third-Party Services

iMeasure may use third-party services to provide authentication and application functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

iMeasure currently uses Supabase for optional authentication and related backend functionality.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by iMeasure. Network communication with supported online services is transmitted using encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to provide features of the application, such as optional authentication.

9. Your Choices

You can use the core iMeasure application without signing in.

You can also control device permissions, including camera, location and sensor-related permissions, through your device settings.

10. Account Deletion

If you create an account and want it deleted, please contact us using the contact information below. We will provide instructions for requesting deletion of your account and associated account information.

11. Children's Privacy

iMeasure is not directed specifically toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
