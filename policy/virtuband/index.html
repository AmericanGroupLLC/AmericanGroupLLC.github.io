Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

The short version. VirtuBand works offline. Your instruments, song sections and recordings stay on your device. We do not sell your personal information, we do not show third-party ads, and you can use every feature without creating an account.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the VirtuBand mobile application (“VirtuBand”, “the app”, “we”) handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the VirtuBand app published under the package name com.americangroupllc.virtuband.

1. Using VirtuBand without an account

Signing in is optional. You can tap Continue as Guest and use every core feature — the studio, all eight instruments, song sections, AI Compose, jam sessions, recordings and settings — without providing an email address or any other personal information.

If you later choose to sign in, you can do so from Settings → Account. Signing out returns you to guest mode; it does not lock you out of the app.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is saved in local storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us.

WhatWhy
Language preferenceTo show the app in your chosen language
Onboarding & guest statusSo you are not shown onboarding or the sign-in screen again
Song sections (name, tempo, key, bars)To restore your arrangement when you reopen the app
Recording metadata (title, duration, date, tempo, genre)To list your saved takes
Audio recordingsSaved as files in the app's private storage so you can play them back
Signed-in email address (only if you sign in)To restore your session

Uninstalling the app removes this data. You can delete individual recordings at any time from the Recordings screen.

3. Microphone

VirtuBand requests the RECORD_AUDIO permission only when you first start a recording. Microphone audio is used solely to capture the takes you explicitly start, is written to your device's private app storage, and is never uploaded by the app. If you decline the permission, every other feature continues to work — you simply cannot record.

4. Account & authentication

If you choose to create or use an account, authentication is handled by Supabase, which processes your email address and an encrypted password on our behalf in order to verify your identity and maintain your session. We do not use your email for marketing, and we do not sell or rent it.

This is the only feature that requires an internet connection and the only reason the app declares the INTERNET permission.

5. Analytics

VirtuBand records a small number of anonymous, aggregate interaction counts (for example, that playback was started or that the language was changed) to understand which features are used. These events are held in memory for the current session only, contain no personal identifiers, and contain no audio. This build does not integrate a third-party analytics SDK.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not display third-party advertising and do not use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not upload your recordings, arrangements or microphone audio.
  • We do not track your location. The app requests no location permission.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, calendar, SMS or call logs.
  • We do not use third-party trackers or social-media SDKs.

7. Children's privacy

VirtuBand is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at contact@safecodeg.com and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Because the app stores your content locally and collects no personal information from guests, most data is under your direct control: delete recordings in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

If you created an account, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the email address associated with it — including the right to withdraw consent, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability where applicable under laws such as the GDPR and CCPA/CPRA. Email contact@safecodeg.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Data retention

On-device data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the app. Account records, if you created an account, are retained until you request deletion.

10. Security

Account credentials are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and we never store your password in the app. Your recordings remain in the app's private storage area, which other apps cannot read. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International transfers

If you use an account, the authentication provider may process your email address on servers outside your country. Where required, appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new “Last updated” date on this page. Continuing to use the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
